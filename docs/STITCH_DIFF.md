# STITCH_DIFF.md

This document compares the design system and layouts generated by Stitch MCP against our exact measurements from the `DESIGN_AUDIT.md`.

*Note: Per instructions, `DESIGN_AUDIT.md` remains the absolute source of truth. No tokens have been changed to match Stitch.*

## 1. Colors & Surfaces
- **Audit:** Clean `#ffffff` canvas with `#efeeec` (light grey) and `#2a2928` (black) high-contrast blocking.
- **Stitch:** Generated a more complex, tonal Material-style palette. It introduced a warm off-white surface (`#fbf9f5`) and darker primary variants (`#6d5e00`).
- **Verdict:** **Do not adopt.** The pure white and strict greyscale background blocking from the audit provides a much sharper, high-end editorial feel than the warmed tonal palette.

## 2. Typography
- **Audit:** `Aspekta` font family. H1 fluidly scales to `84.48px` on desktop with a very tight `78.57px` line-height (0.93x).
- **Stitch:** Substituted `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Created a standard 84px `display-xl` token, but with a `92px` line-height (1.1x). 
- **Verdict:** **Do not adopt.** The tighter line-height found in the Webflow template is crucial for giving the massive headlines their confident, interlocking architectural look. We will keep our fluid `clamp()` and tight leading.

## 3. Spacing & Layout Rhythm
- **Audit:** `160px` top/bottom section padding, `24px` grid gaps.
- **Stitch:** Perfectly adopted the `margin: 10rem` (160px) and `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px) constraints from the brief.
- **Verdict:** **N/A** (Matched).

## 4. Components & Depth (Shadows)
- **Audit:** Flat, structural design. Depth is inferred via 1px `#e2e0dc` borders rather than heavy drop shadows.
- **Stitch:** Recommended an "ultra-diffused ambient shadow" (`0 16px 40px -12px rgba(42, 41, 40, 0.05)`).
- **Verdict:** **Worth adopting for interactive states.** Our audit noted "INFERRED shadow" on cards during hover states. Stitch's highly specific, ultra-diffused shadow fits the premium aesthetic perfectly and is better than a default Tailwind `shadow-xl`.

## 5. Visual Motif (Dots)
- **Audit:** Repeated dot motif, measured as SVG elements with `#fbe57e` fills.
- **Stitch:** Codified the dot motif into a structural rule: "1.5px circles on 16px centers in subtle stone neutral".
- **Verdict:** **Worth adopting conceptually.** Formalizing the dot matrix sizing ensures we don't scale it randomly across sections. We will implement the dots at a fixed `w-1.5 h-1.5` scale rather than letting them scale fluidly.
